A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Meet your
new matcha.

Everything you need to know before you crack your first can.

What is the Discovery Pack?

A proposed first-order starter: six cans—two Lychee, two Peach, and two Strawberry—for $19 with shipping included. The assortment, price, eligible regions, taxes, and fulfillment feasibility are not approved. This prototype cannot accept orders.

How would the taste guarantee work?

The proposal is simple: love your first pack or contact us for a refund. This is not a current policy. Eligibility, request window, refund scope, and any return requirements must be approved and displayed before launch.

Does Tatsu taste like matcha?

Tatsu is made with matcha, but the experience is light, refreshing, and fruit-forward—not a traditional earthy matcha drink. Choose Lychee, Peach, or Strawberry.

What’s inside a can?

Japanese matcha blended with real fruit juice, electrolytes, vitamins, and minerals. Tatsu is non-carbonated. The Lychee label lists 10 calories, 2 g total sugars, and 0 g added sugars per 12 fl oz can; its product page lists 52 mg caffeine. Peach and Strawberry values still need flavor-specific confirmation. See each flavor’s label for full ingredients.

How do I drink it?

Enjoy it chilled, straight from the can or poured over ice. No mixing, whisking, or brewing required.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. The proposed Discovery Pack would be a one-time purchase, with no subscription required. For existing full cases, choose a one-time 24-can purchase for $68, or a monthly subscription for $59.99 per delivery. Subscriptions renew automatically; the current store states that you can modify or cancel anytime. Confirm final terms on the live product page.

When will my order arrive?

The proposed starter price includes shipping, but the eligible destinations, dispatch time, delivery window, and tax treatment still need approval. This demo does not accept orders.

Where can I find ingredients and nutrition?

Review the flavor-by-flavor information here, or visit the live product page for the current label. View the live store ↗

DESIGN DIRECTION / V5

Built to sell the first sip.

Proposed Shopify homepage direction—not a live theme or published store change. Desktop and mobile layouts are included.

A stronger direct-response hierarchy

Inspired by Brad’s reference: one bold promise, a large delivered-price lockup, one high-contrast primary CTA, a compact proposed guarantee, and a genuine testimonial excerpt immediately below. No fabricated ratings, clinical endorsements, crossed-out prices, savings claims, or deadlines. Tatsu keeps its original logo and matcha-first positioning.

The Everyday Dose inspiration

  • One focused hero: Japanese matcha is the core value proposition. Energy and focus lead; electrolytes, convenience, and fruit flavor support it.
  • Shopping, sooner: flavor selection, pack and per-can pricing, and one-time versus subscription options appear before the longer brand story.
  • Benefits → proof → purchase: scannable ingredient benefits, existing testimonials, FAQs, and repeated shopping CTAs.
  • Mobile-first conversion: large tap targets, simplified navigation, and a persistent welcome-offer CTA.

What stays distinctly Tatsu

The original logo supplied by Brad, current product images, and bright matcha-green photography. No Everyday Dose images, logos, offers, guarantees, or review counts have been reused.

The Discovery Pack proposal

Lead with one easy first purchase: six cans across available flavors for an illustrative $19 delivered, no subscription, and a proposed first-order taste guarantee. Matcha remains the core value proposition; a transparent trial price reduces purchase friction. No deadline, fake stock count, or unverified saving is used.

Not an existing offer: confirm bundle feasibility, assortment, margins after fulfillment and shipping, taxes, eligible regions, first-order limits, refund scope and window, and abuse controls. Approve the guarantee before publishing. Do not stack the existing GetAvid benefit by default. This prototype collects no email and processes no payment.

Source-checked pricing

Product pages reviewed October 16, 2026 showed $68 one-time and $59.99 monthly for 24 cans, saving $8.01. Per-can prices are rounded and shown before any welcome offer. One-time purchase is the default. Confirm final pricing, stock, selling plans, and checkout totals in Shopify.

Before this goes live

  • Client approves copy, offer economics, imagery, claims, testimonials, and any required endorsement disclosures.
  • Connect real Shopify variants, cart, discount rules, and existing subscription app. Preserve existing email flows.
  • Confirm ingredients, nutrition, stock, delivery estimates, and subscription terms. The Discovery Pack, free shipping, and taste guarantee are proposals only; approve their economics and policies before launch.
  • Validate GetAvid redemption, explicit marketing consent, Klaviyo sync, and coupon-message overlap. Avoid conflicting discounts or multiple overlapping popups.
  • Test mobile offer-to-checkout flow, accessibility, page speed, Meta events, value/currency, and browser/server deduplication with a controlled order.
